    Story of this worker

    Built around the worker: respect first

    Gulf sites bring together workers from many countries, languages, faiths and habits. Most left home to earn for their families, so the fear of losing pay can make a worker hide symptoms. HeatWatch speaks their language, says plainly that heat breaks are paid, and requires supervisors to be trained before they go on site.

    ⚓ Ports🛢 Offshore🏗 Construction🚚 Logistics
    People decideRules raise the alert; a supervisor and the site medic decide. AI only puts it in plain words, in the worker's language.
    Mood is human, and privateAn optional daily check-in (good / OK / tired / stressed, sleep). Only the worker sees the answer. A hard day can only bring an earlier break, never a looser limit.
    The worker chooses the toneGentle or direct words for everyday messages. RED is always short, firm and clear, and cannot be muted. Firm, never rude.
    No blame, no resetMissed check-ins get a friendly welcome back; progress is never reset to zero. Several low days offer confidential support to the worker only.
    Never used against workersNot for discipline, performance rating, hiring or insurance. Supervisors see colours, not health data or mood.
    Measured over timeLead time, false alarms, check-in rate, recovery after lapses and equal benefit across crews, nationalities and languages, reported anonymously.
